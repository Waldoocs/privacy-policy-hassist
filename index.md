# Privacy Policy for Home Assistant rclone Integration

This is a personal tool used to manage files between Home Assistant and Google Drive using rclone. We do not collect or share any personal data through this integration. All data stays on your own devices, and we only access the Google Drive account that you grant permission to during the OAuth process.

No data is stored on any external servers or shared with third parties.

If you have any concerns about privacy, please contact us at [kenanburakkenan@gmail.com].
